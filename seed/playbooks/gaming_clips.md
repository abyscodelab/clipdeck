# Category Playbook: Gaming (FPS, MOBA, Battle Royale, Speedruns)

## Scope & Target
- **Scope**: Category
- **Target**: Gaming
- **Priority**: High

## What to Clip (Viral Patterns)
- **Clutch Plays**: 1v3+ situations, buzzer beaters, impossible defuses, last-second headshots.
- **Rage & Desk Slams**: Streamer visibly raging, slamming peripherals, exasperated reactions to bugs or bad teammates.
- **Embarrassing Whiffs / Fails**: Missing point-blank shots, self-grenades, falling off maps, dying to environmental hazards.
- **Unexpected Glitches / Physics**: Hilarious game bugs, physics anomalies, ragdoll moments.
- **Hot Takes on Game Balance**: Strong opinions on weapon nerfs, patch notes, or esports drama that trigger chat debate.
- **Chat Copypasta Bursts**: Moments where chat spam velocity exceeds 30 msg/s with gaming emotes (OMEGALUL, KEKW, PepeHands, PogChamp).

## Hard Rules (What NOT to Clip)
- **NO Loading Screens**: Cut immediately if game is loading or queuing.
- **NO Long Inventory Management**: Never include more than 1.5 seconds of menu navigation or looting.
- **NO Muted Audio / Mic Cutouts**: Do not clip if streamer audio is dead or distorted.
- **NO Minors / Harassment**: Strictly avoid toxicity targeting underage players or lobby voice chat slurs.
- **NO Copyrighted DMCA Music**: Flag if background music matches DMCA danger lists (radio stations in GTA, streamer playlist reads).

## Pacing & Editing Rules
- **Duration**: 18 to 45 seconds (ideal sweet spot: 28 seconds).
- **Hook Time**: First audio punch or gunshot must happen within 1.5 seconds.
- **Dead-Air Cut Threshold**: Cut any silence or gameplay dead-air greater than 0.5 seconds.
- **Layout Recommendation**: `facecam_split` (top 30% streamer facecam, bottom 70% gameplay) or `blurred_bg` with 1.15x scale gameplay center.
- **Visual Effects**: 
  - Zoom-punch (1.2x) on streamer face reaction when eliminated or winning.
  - Subtitle styling: bold yellow/white popping font with stroke.

## Platform Metadata Guidelines
- **Title Hook**: Always lead with emotion or action in caps ("HE ACTUALLY HIT THAT?!", "THE GREATEST 1v4 ACE").
- **Hashtags**: `#gaming #twitchclips #gamer #clutch #rage #highlight #esports #clipdeck`
- **Credit**: Always tag `@streamer_handle` in description.
