# Viral Moments & Brand-Safe Rules

## What to Clip (Viral Patterns)
- Rage moments: streamer loses it, slams desk, yells
- Unexpected fails: embarrassing deaths, whiffs, blunders
- Clutch plays: impossible saves, ace rounds, buzzer beaters
- Wholesome moments: heartfelt donations, emotional reactions
- Hot takes: controversial opinions that spark chat
- "Chat went crazy": any moment where chat velocity 3x+ baseline
- Collab moments: funny interactions between streamers
- Reaction clips: streamer reacting to something wild

## What NOT to Clip (Hard Rules)
- NO gambling segments or sponsor reads
- NO clips with minors visible on camera
- NO copyrighted music as the main audio (background game music is OK)
- NO slurs, hate speech, or discriminatory content
- NO personal information visible on screen (doxxing risk)
- NO content the streamer has explicitly asked to keep off-clip
- NO technical difficulties, loading screens, or AFK segments

## Editing Style
- Keep clips between 15-60 seconds
- Hook must land in the first 2 seconds
- Cut all dead air over 0.7 seconds
- Prefer fast pacing over completeness
- Include 2-3 seconds of context before the moment
- Include the chat/audience reaction after the moment

## Caption & Title Rules
- Tone: casual, hyperbolic, emoji-heavy
- Always credit streamer: 🎥 @{streamer_handle}
- Use 5-8 hashtags per platform
- No clickbait that misrepresents the clip content
- Capitalize key words in titles for emphasis
