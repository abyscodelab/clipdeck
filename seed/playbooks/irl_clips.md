# Category Playbook: IRL & Outdoor Streaming

## Scope & Target
- **Scope**: Category
- **Target**: IRL
- **Priority**: High

## What to Clip (Viral Patterns)
- **Stranger & Public Interactions**: Surprising encounters, funny bystander reactions, comedic banter with food vendors/street performers.
- **Wholesome & Emotional Moments**: Fans meeting the streamer, heartfelt donations, acts of kindness.
- **Travel / Navigation Blunders**: Getting lost, ordering unfamiliar dishes, language miscommunications.
- **Microphone / Wind Spikes**: Hilarious physical comedy or sudden jumpscares in outdoor environments.
- **Street Collaborations**: Spontaneous guest appearances and streamer crossovers.

## Hard Rules (What NOT to Clip)
- **NO Doxxing or Private Information**: Strict ban on visible credit cards, phone screens, boarding passes, license plates, hotel room numbers, or street addresses.
- **NO Unconsenting Confrontations**: Do not clip aggressive public arguments or incidents where individuals demand not to be filmed.
- **NO Filming Minors**: Immediately cut any segment prominently featuring children without explicit parental authorization.
- **NO Illegal Activities**: Zero tolerance for traffic infractions, trespassing, or public disturbance clips.
- **NO Medical or Emergency Situations**: Never clip sirens, accidents, or distress calls.

## Pacing & Editing Rules
- **Duration**: 20 to 55 seconds.
- **Hook Time**: Vocal hook or visual cue within 2.0 seconds.
- **Dead-Air Cut Threshold**: Cut pedestrian walking or silence exceeding 0.7 seconds.
- **Layout Recommendation**: `blurred_bg` with full horizontal frame in center, or smart `face_track` if centered on person speaking.
- **Captions**: Dynamic animated captions with speaker differentiation (Streamer: cyan/yellow, Bystander: white).

## Platform Metadata Guidelines
- **Title Hook**: Casual, relatable tone with situational irony ("When in Tokyo...", "He had no idea who he was talking to 💀").
- **Hashtags**: `#irl #travel #streamer #funny #wholesome #publicreactions #clipdeck`
- **Credit**: Include location tag and `@streamer_handle`.
